# Quality evaluation - NVIDIA GeForce RTX 4060 Laptop GPU

Reference: Sintel trailer (c) Blender Foundation | durian.blender.org, CC BY 3.0; 32.5s + 6.0s, 1920x816 (letterbox removed).
Higher is better for PSNR / SSIM / VMAF; lower is better for LPIPS and flicker (temporal noise in static regions, luma levels). PSNR uses MSE pooled over all frames. Seam ratio: frame-to-frame change at chunk boundaries / elsewhere, divided by the same ratio of the chunk-free bicubic baseline at those frames (~1.0 = invisible; the spec flags > 1.2).
